Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

MyBrandFather ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Technical data. Our hosting provider automatically logs standard technical information (IP address, browser type, pages requested) to operate the site securely.

We use your information to respond to inquiries, prepare quotes, deliver projects, and — only with your consent — share occasional updates. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

How long we keep it

We retain project and correspondence records for as long as needed to serve you and to meet reasonable business, legal, and accounting obligations, then delete or anonymize them.

Your choices

You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it (subject to legal requirements) by emailing support@mybrandfather.com.

Children

Our services are intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy
