Questions

Answered before you ask.

How much does a logo or brand identity cost?

Logos start at $499 and complete brand identities at $1,499 — fixed pricing, quoted up front, with everything listed before we begin. Full packages are published openly on our pricing page.

How fast can you deliver?

First logo concepts in 5 business days on standard projects; most logo projects finish inside 2–3 weeks. Working to a launch date? Tell us your deadline — rush options exist.

Is the work really custom?

Yes. Brand and design work is created from scratch by our human team — no templates. Our AI services are configured and trained specifically for your business, then designed to match the brand we build. Original, and exclusively yours.

What if I don't like the first designs?

Revision rounds are built into every package, and Professional and above include unlimited revisions. On Starter, if the first round misses, we re-brief and redo it once, free.

Do I own the final files?

Completely. Full copyright transfer and every source file — AI, EPS, SVG, PNG, PDF — with no licensing fees, ever.
