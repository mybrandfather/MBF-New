About us

Your brand, your website, your AI — one team behind all of it.

MyBrandFather began with a simple observation: growing businesses were stuck choosing between cheap templated design that made them look small, and big-agency fees that made professional branding impossible. We built the third option — a senior creative team, fixed transparent pricing, and craftsmanship in everything we ship.

Why the name? A good father sets you up to succeed and stands behind you. That's the role we play for your business — building what you need to look established, and staying in your corner as you grow.

We're a small senior team based in Manchester, Connecticut, working with clients everywhere. Every project is led by an experienced designer or developer — never handed down.

What we believe

Craft over shortcuts

Outcomes over deliverables

A logo that earns trust, a site that converts, an AI that books the appointment. Pretty is how it ships; results are the point.

Partnership over transactions

We measure ourselves on one thing: whether you'd come back for your next project.
