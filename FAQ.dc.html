Frequently asked questions.

How does pricing work?

Every project gets a fixed, written quote before we start — based on scope, not hours. You'll always know the total cost up front, with no surprise invoices along the way.

How long does a typical project take?

A brand identity usually takes 2–3 weeks; a full website 4–8 weeks depending on complexity; AI systems 1–3 weeks once your process is mapped out. We give you a specific timeline in your quote, not a vague estimate.

Who owns the final files and code?

You do — fully. Once a project is paid in full, all source files, code, and rights transfer to you. We don't hold your brand hostage behind a subscription.

Can the AI systems actually be trusted to talk to my customers?

Yes — every AI chatbot and voice agent we build is trained specifically on your business, tested before launch, and configured to hand off to a human whenever a caller or visitor asks for one. Nothing goes live without your review.

What if I just want a premium template, not a custom build?

Our Templates Marketplace has ready-made dashboards, websites, and UI kits you can preview live and launch immediately. If you later want it customized to your brand, we can do that too.

Do you work with businesses outside the US?

Yes — MyBrandFather has worked with clients around the world since 2012. We're comfortable coordinating across time zones and languages.

Do you offer 3D product renders and social media ads?

Yes — 3D product visualization and social media ad creative are both part of our standard capabilities, alongside branding, web, illustration, and AI systems. See "What We Create" on the homepage for the full list.

How many revisions are included?

Every project includes structured revision rounds, agreed to in your quote before work begins, so there's never ambiguity about what's covered.

What happens after launch?

We stay reachable for support, small updates, and questions — we're a small family studio, not a call center, so you'll always be talking to someone who actually worked on your project.

How do I get started?

Reach out through our contact page with a short description of your goals — we reply personally within one business day with next steps and a fixed quote.
