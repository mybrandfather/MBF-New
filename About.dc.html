Our story

A family business, built on craft.

MyBrandFather isn't a holding company or a franchise of contractors. It's a family — siblings who grew up around a paintbrush before we ever touched a keyboard.

Where it started

Our father was the first artist we knew.

Long before MyBrandFather existed, our father was a working artist — the kind of person who could look at a blank page and see exactly what belonged on it. Growing up, our house was full of sketches, half-finished canvases, and the quiet discipline of someone who took craft seriously. We didn't choose to love design so much as inherit it. Art came into our blood early, and it never really left.

As kids, each of us gravitated toward a different piece of that inheritance — drawing, illustration, layout, color, storytelling. It meant that by the time we were old enough to work, we already had an instinct for what makes something feel considered instead of thrown together.

Founded in 2012

Siblings, working together, since 2012.

MyBrandFather started in 2012 as freelance design work between siblings — logos, packaging, illustration, brand identities — taken on for clients scattered across the world. There was no office, no big pitch deck, just a shared inbox and a family that had grown up trusting each other's eye. Over the years that freelance work became a real practice: real clients, real deadlines, real reputations to protect.

Working with clients internationally taught us something a local studio rarely learns as fast: how to communicate clearly across time zones, cultures, and industries, and how to make a brand feel right no matter where in the world it needs to land.

Learning to build, not just design

I didn't start out knowing how to code.

As the elder brother, I didn't come into this with any background in coding, AI, or marketing. I could design a brand, but I couldn't yet build the website that would carry it, or make sure anyone found it once it launched. So over the past several years, I taught myself — websites, SEO, AI automation, digital marketing — the same way our father taught himself to paint: by doing the work, getting it wrong, and doing it again until it was right.

Along the way, I started helping other freelancers do the same thing — untangling their websites, fixing their SEO, showing them how AI tools could take repetitive work off their plate. That experience shaped how MyBrandFather operates today: we don't just hand a client a logo and disappear. We stay close to the technical and strategic side of the business too, because we've had to learn it ourselves, the hard way.

MyBrandFather today

One family. One roof. Every discipline you need.

Today MyBrandFather is a small, senior team of siblings and close collaborators covering branding, web design, UI/UX, illustration, 3D product visualization, AI systems, and premium templates — all under one roof, all held to the same standard our father held his own work to.

We're still a family business. That means every project gets someone who genuinely cares whether it succeeds — not a rotating account manager, but people who built this studio from freelance beginnings and have every reason to protect what it's become.
